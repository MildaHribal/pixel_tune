⚠ Changing the resolution is risky
A non-native resolution can break the layout of system elements and of some apps. After switching, a 60 s safeguard runs: if you do not press Confirm within that time, the system returns to native 1080 × 2400 by itself. If the UI cannot be operated after the change, wait a minute and the resolution comes back on its own.
The native preset is applied without a safeguard. The reset sets DPI 420.

What is genuinely per-app

PER-APP the FPS limit and downscale — these are held by the system Game Mode at the app itself. They apply always while that app runs and do not touch any other app. This is real isolation.

GLOBAL the performance profile, GPU and resolution — the phone can only have these set once for the whole system. pixel_tune therefore merely switches them when you enter an app and switches them back when you leave. While the app is in the foreground they affect the entire system, including everything running in the background.

So do not expect isolation that does not exist: two apps running with two different profiles at once is not possible. The one in the foreground wins, and with fast switching the change may lag a little behind.
